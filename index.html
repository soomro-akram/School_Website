About the school

Six decades of teaching Padidan's sons

GBHSS Padidan is a government-run institution offering education from Class 6 to Intermediate (Part I & II), affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Sindh.

Our Mission

To provide free, quality secondary and higher-secondary education to the boys of Padidan and its neighbouring villages, and to prepare disciplined, capable citizens for higher study and public service.

Our Vision

A school where every student who walks through our gates — regardless of household income — leaves with the grades, discipline, and confidence to pursue college, university, or a trade of his choosing.

Affiliation

Registered with the Government of Sindh, School Education & Literacy Department, and affiliated with BISE Larkana for Matriculation and Intermediate examinations.

Principal's Message

"For over sixty years this school has stood at the centre of Padidan — not merely as a building, but as a promise to every family here that their sons will have the chance to learn, compete, and rise. We hold ourselves to that promise every single day: in the classroom, on the sports field, and in how we treat every boy who walks through our gate."

Academics

Classes & streams offered

From Class 6 through Intermediate Part II, with dedicated Science and Humanities streams taught by subject specialists.

Secondary (IV–X)

The Matriculation years — building core fundamentals in preparation for the Board examination.

  • Classes 6 To 8: English, Urdu, Sindhi, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Islamiat
  • Science Group — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths
  • Compulsory: Urdu, Sindhi, English, Islamiyat

Intermediate — Pre-Medical

For students pursuing MBBS, BDS, Pharm-D and allied health science programmes.

  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • English & Compulsory Studies
  • MDCAT preparation support

Intermediate — Pre-Engineering

For students headed toward engineering, computer science, and technology degrees.

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
  • Computer Science (elective)
  • ECAT preparation support

Admissions

Admission to Class 6 and Intermediate Part I opens each year in July–August. Follow the steps below, or submit your interest online.

01

Collect the admission form

Available free of cost from the school office during working hours (8 AM – 2 PM, Monday–Saturday).

02

Submit required documents

Previous school leaving certificate, B-Form/CNIC copy, two passport-size photographs, and previous mark sheet.

03

Verification & interview

A short interview with the admission committee to confirm eligibility and place the student in the correct stream.

04

Confirm enrolment

Once approved, complete registration at the school office. Education at GBHSS Padidan is free, as with all Sindh government schools.
